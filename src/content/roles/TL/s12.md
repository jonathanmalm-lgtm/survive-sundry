Twenty minutes after second service. Everyone is standing. Nobody has eaten since the huddle donuts four hours ago. This is not a meeting with food. This is the meeting where you find out what people actually thought.

The campus pastor goes first. Hit: both services ran. Miss: he spent turnover in the lobby and missed his window to check in with the team. He looks your way.

Before you can speak, Tom the head usher says, "I just want to be honest — I felt like tech had some *moments* today." He says it with the gentle sincerity of a man certain he is helping. You have lived a day that contained a dying subwoofer, a meme about Pastor Dave on a twelve-foot screen, a child you taught to lick a battery, a stranger who toured with DC Talk in an undisclosed capacity, and a $200 crypto position backed by Blippi. You nod slowly at Tom. Inside, you are on a beach you have never visited.

## A

The room goes quiet by item three. The campus pastor stops writing by item five. Greg, if he stayed, closes his notebook. When you finish, the campus pastor asks, "What do you need from me?" You have a list. You have always had a list. You hand him the list.

## B

Clean. Confident. The room sees a person who clearly runs something. Tom nods along in a way that quietly suggests he is taking partial credit for the wins. You do not look at Tom. Looking at Tom is how Tom gets you.

## C

One item. You name the subwoofer, attach a solution, and stop talking. The campus pastor nods. Greg nods. Tom opens his mouth. The campus pastor raises one hand a few inches in Tom's direction. Tom closes his mouth. You file the gesture away in the place where you keep useful things.

## D:win

"There was actually some RF interference bleeding onto the usher comms channel," you say, "from an unmuted cell phone near the back. Threw the whole low end off." You look directly at Tom as you say *unmuted cell phone.* Tom does not know what RF is. To be fully honest, your own grasp of it is more vibe than science. But Tom's hand drifts to his pocket. Tom is now reviewing his entire morning. Tom does not speak again. Next Sunday Tom arrives thirty minutes early with his phone in airplane mode and a haunted look. You have weaponized a word.

## D:lose

You deliver the RF theory with total confidence. Unfortunately, Greg is still in the room, and Greg ran a full Dante network with fiber runs, and Greg knows that is not even slightly how any of that works. Greg says nothing. Greg does not have to. Pastor Dave tilts his head and asks, mildly, "Did you just make up a word?" "It's a real term," you say, which is true, and also not the point, and everyone can tell it's not the point.

Around the room: the worship leader reports second service "had something." Kids ministry describes a bathroom situation in eleven words and the room receives them in silence. First Impressions says the door felt warm but the lobby felt cold; nobody claims the lobby. Someone says the haze was "a lot today." Nobody claims the haze.

"Same time next week."

Four seconds.

Tom moves first.
