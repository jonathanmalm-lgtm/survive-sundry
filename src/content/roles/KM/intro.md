Your alarm goes off at 6:15. You've been awake since 5:50. That's fine. You use the extra time to review your curriculum notes, which you memorized on Thursday, and then you review them again.

There is a craft supply shortage in the third-grade room that you are aware of and are choosing to be calm about. You are mostly calm about it. You are calm about it the way a person is calm about a sound their car is making.

Your check-in system pushed an update overnight and you have not yet confirmed whether the name-tag labels are printing correctly. They are probably printing correctly. You will confirm this before any child arrives, because a child has never arrived at Execution Church Kids Ministry without a properly formatted name tag, and today will not be the first time. Name tags are not a suggestion. Name tags are how you find a parent when a two-year-old will not say their own name.

You're in the car by 6:38. You'll be at church by 6:55. That gives you five extra minutes to confirm the labels, and four extra minutes after that to panic about something you haven't thought of yet.
